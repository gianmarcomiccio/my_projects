Here is the collection of the lectures I created for ReLU, the machine learning association at NTNU.

The lessons are aimed at students with different backgrounds (CS, Mathematics, Physics, Industrial Economics...) and with different levels of knowledge

They include:
- Statistics and Linear Regression (from mean vector and covariance matrix to all diagnostics with linear regression)
- Bias and Variance tradeoff
- Time Series and how to treat them, with Neural Networks, RNNs and LSTMs. It includes a full notebook that was used to guide the lecture and code live
- Classification models, from logistic regression to decision tree models
- Cross Validation and Hyperparameter Optimization for ML models
